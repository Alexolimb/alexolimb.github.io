Free-model brain (Zero / Altron)

An assistant’s brain assembled entirely from free models: eight local ones plus free access, an agent with 33 tools, memory, and search over a personal knowledge base.

FMBDevelopment frozen since 31 Jul 2026 and not re-checked since — said plainly rather than left to look current.

What it is

An assistant living on a paid interface has a permanent cost and dies together with the balance. The opposite arrangement is built here: several free sources with roles and fallbacks, where a local model always closes the chain. If a free quota runs out or an external service goes down, the assistant gets weaker and keeps working.

A brain is not just a model. There is also an agent with 33 tools — that is, with real actions behind them; memory between conversations; and search over a personal knowledge base, so that an answer can quote your own documents.

Specifications: Node.js, eight local models plus the Groq free tier, and knowledge-base search. Stated plainly: development has been frozen since 31 Jul 2026 and has not been re-checked since — the work stands here as it was left.
