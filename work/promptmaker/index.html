PromptMaker — one sentence in, a professional prompt out

Say what you need in plain words and get a finished prompt for any of 13 AI tools, written by that particular tool’s rules.

What it is

Every AI tool has its own prompt dialect: what produces a good result in one gives rubbish in another, because it expects a different word order, different keys and a different length. A person who just wants a picture or a clip should not have to know thirteen of those dialects.

The program translates between them: in goes an ordinary sentence in your own language, out comes one finished prompt assembled by the chosen tool’s rules. That rulebook, 185 KB of it, lives inside the program itself: it works with no internet and does not depend on whether somebody else’s service is up today.

Specifications: a desktop application in Electron, with the installer and the icon built, and a Russian interface. Where it stopped is stated plainly: parts 7 and 8 — subscription and payment — are frozen. This is a working tool in use rather than a finished product, and it is presented here as exactly that.

What proves it

Usable and tested — 194 tests, installer and icon built. Parts 7-8 (the subscription layer) are paused, so it is a working tool rather than a finished product. Interface is Russian.
