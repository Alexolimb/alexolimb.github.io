WordQuest — English practice that follows you

An English vocabulary trainer for a teacher and their class: an island map with ten game modes, spaced repetition built in, and a separate teacher screen.

What it is

Learning vocabulary only works on repetition to a schedule — and the schedule is the first thing students abandon. So repetition here is built into the game itself instead of sitting in a section of its own: a word that is due comes back inside the next round, and the student meets it without ever choosing “revise”.

The island map sets the order: ten game modes are ten different ways of meeting the same word, from recognising it to typing it from hearing. The teacher has their own screen: they see who is stuck where and build their own word lists around what the class is actually covering.

Specifications: a web application over HTTPS, an always-on-top window, spaced repetition inside. The student side opens without a password; teacher registration is closed behind an invite code. The work is still going, and the list of what is unfinished is short and exact: the level-test screen, importing words from a spreadsheet, and a Mac build. The interface is Russian only for now.
