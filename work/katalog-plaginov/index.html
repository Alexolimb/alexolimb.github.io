A catalogue of 237 Premiere plugins — other people's, not mine

A searchable reference to 237 Premiere plugins and presets — other people’s, not mine — with a column no store has: will this still run in the 2026 version.

What it is

Straight out: this is not my own development. It is an inventory of other people’s plugins, panels, templates and presets — they belong to their authors, and nothing here claims otherwise. It sits on the showcase as a reference that genuinely gets used, not as a piece of work delivered.

What the inventory adds to the stores is one column they do not have: will this run in Premiere 2026. Adobe is burying the old panel engine, some formats stay reliable, others stop opening at all, and the editor finds out only after installing. That is the question in their head while searching for a plugin, and the one no store page answers.

Specifications: a single HTML page with search and filters, no server, no database, no build step — it opens on a double click and works offline. Compiled and checked on 13 Aug 2026.
