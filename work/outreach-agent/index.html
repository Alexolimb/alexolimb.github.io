Outreach Agent — an automated lead pipeline (retired)

A cold-outreach agent that measures a business’s own site and brings a letter about one specific weakness for signature — with a number in it, not an opinion.

No screenshots: they would show other people's data
OA

What it is

Cold emails fail for an obvious reason: they are all the same. The recipient has seen that letter a hundred times and recognises the template from the first line. The only thing that distinguishes a letter that gets a reply is a specific fact about the recipient that they can check and will admit is true.

So the agent measures first and writes second: it visits the business’s site in a real browser, takes the readings and picks one specific weakness out of them. The letter carries the measurement rather than a judgement: “your page takes 6.2 seconds to open on a phone” instead of “your site could be better”. Two rules were never up for discussion: the address is taken only from their own website, never from a purchased list, and nothing goes out without a human signing it off.

Specifications: Node.js, measurements taken by a headless browser, wiring on n8n, sign-off through Telegram. Switched off on 10 Aug 2026 along with the rest of the commercial track. No screenshots: the screens hold other companies’ contact details.

What proves it

Built and proven (227 tests, two real letters sent); switched off on 10 Aug 2026.
