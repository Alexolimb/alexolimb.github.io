Dayo — planning a day out loud

A day planner you talk to: say everything you need to do and the AI lays it out by the 1-3-5 rule, leaving the day possible.

What it is

To-do lists break on the same thing every time: everything that came to mind goes in, and by evening half of it is undone. The problem is volume, and discipline has nothing to do with it: the day physically does not hold what was written into it. The app is built around that: it does not help you fit more in, it stops you writing down more than fits.

It works like this: you speak the day out loud, speech becomes text, and the model lays out what was said by the 1-3-5 rule — one big task, three medium, five small — and by the sixty-per-cent rule: no more than sixty per cent of the working time goes into the plan, the rest is left for whatever happens. After that come repeats and reminders.

Specifications: Flutter, an SQLite database on the phone itself — the entries go nowhere and the app works offline. The Android package is built but has not been published to a store. The limits are stated plainly: the interface is Russian only for now, and the AI tab needs a server address that is not configured on the machine shown — which is why “speak your day” is not demonstrated live in the screenshots.

What proves it

Working, 69 tests, Android build ready, not in any store. Interface is Russian only. The AI tab needs a server address that is not configured on this machine, so the speech-to-plan step has not been demonstrated live.
