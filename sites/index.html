Sites people open and use

A live site is not a picture in a portfolio, it is an address that opens right now. Everything here can be touched without leaving this tab: a real commission, eight samples of my own for different trades, and this site itself.

The samples are deliberately not built from one template. A dental clinic, an app landing page and a working console are solving different problems: the first has to take away fear, the second has to explain a product in one screen, the third has to stay out of the way of somebody working eight hours in it. One identical page would have solved one problem out of three.

All eight are static builds: no database, no server, no content system — a page like that drops onto any file host, opens instantly and has nothing to break. Seven of them live inside this site, each with its own 3D scene computed on your graphics card: embers over coals, a district turning under the camera, a wheel drawn in ink, drops merging on glass, a spectrogram running into the distance, a field of half-hour cells, three ribbons weaving. Loud where the trade itself is loud, barely there where somebody works all day. The eighth, a cafe, is published on its own address and built the same way. Five of the eight are in English and three in Spanish, because a local business in Spain deserves its own language.
