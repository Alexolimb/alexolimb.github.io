Questions we always get

I have never spoken a word. Can I still start?

Yes, and it is the normal case here: half of every A1 group starts without a word. What we will not do is put you in a group above your level just to fill it.

What happens if I miss a class?

Nothing. We send you what was covered and you can take that class again with another group at the same level within the month. A single missed class is not refunded, and we say so before charging you.

Is there an enrolment fee?

No. No enrolment fee, no materials fee, no “platform charges”. The price in the table is the price.

And if I leave the city mid-course?

Fifteen days’ notice and that is that. No lock-in: people living abroad move, and charging for that would be taking advantage.

First class, free
